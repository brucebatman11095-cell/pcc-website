Why this exists

We built this for our own shop first.

We run a press shop. For years the honest answer to "how many pieces did we make today?" was a supervisor's notebook, three WhatsApp groups, and a bit of guessing. Every ERP we looked at was either built for a factory ten times our size, or stopped working the moment the internet dropped — which, on a shop floor, is most weeks.

So we made the thing we actually needed: something an operator can use without training, that tracks a job from the order sheet all the way to the dispatch challan, and that keeps working on a dusty PC with no connection. Once it was running in our own plant, other shops started asking for it.

It's not the biggest system out there. It's the one that was built by people who've stood next to the press.
