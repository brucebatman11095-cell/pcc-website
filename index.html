Most shops run on memory, WhatsApp messages, and a supervisor's notebook. It works — until it doesn't.

No real-time visibility

You find out a machine's been idle for two hours only when someone mentions it at the end of the shift.

Quality data nobody trusts

Inspection sheets exist, but no one can tell you if a dimension is drifting until a customer complains.

Slow jobs, no easy fix

What's inside

Everything from order intake to dispatch, in one place

Built directly from real press-shop and CNC workflows — not adapted from a generic template.

Live production & OEE

Every machine, every job, tracked in real time — availability, performance and quality rolled into one honest OEE number.

QC inspection plans

Define exactly what to measure per item and stage. Mark dimensions Critical — a failure there is a hard reject, no exceptions.

Process capability (SPC)

Cp/Cpk, control charts and a printable capability study — generated automatically from inspections you're already doing.

Configurable documents

Delivery challans and packing lists with the columns you actually need — HSN, box-wise net/gross weight, marks & numbers.

Split a job across machines

Run one big batch on several machines at once — with an honest estimate of whether it's actually faster before you commit.

Team accounts & security

Each person signs in as themselves, restricted to their own department. A one-click self-test proves the isolation actually holds.

Quality that holds up to an audit

Turn inspections you already record into evidence a customer will accept.

No separate data entry. Every inspection your QC team logs feeds straight into a capability study — the kind of document that used to need a consultant.

  • Cp / Cpk / Pp / Ppk calculated automatically, per item and dimension
  • X̄ and R control charts flag drift before it becomes a rejection
  • Honest by design — fewer than 20 inspections is marked "indicative only," never overstated
  • One click prints a customer- and auditor-ready capability report
